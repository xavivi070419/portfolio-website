ABOUT MY FAMILY

  • Mother: Behind every sacrifice my mother made was an unshakeable love that asked for nothing in return. From waking up before dawn to ensure our home was cared for, to carrying the weight of our family’s worries on her own shoulders, her life has been a living testament to selfless devotion. She didn't just nurture us—she gave up her own comfort, poured her heart into every meal, and surrounded us with a protective warmth that made every hardship feel manageable. Her love is the quiet foundation of everything I am, and no matter where life takes me, I carry the depth of her sacrifices and the warmth of her care with endless gratitude.
  • Father: Behind every horizon my father chased as a seafarer was a quiet, profound devotion to our family. Leaving home to face long months across oceans is a sacrifice few truly understand, yet he carried that heavy weight so we could build a better future. Even though distance meant we couldn't see him every day growing up, his presence never faded—his voice, his guidance, and his steady encouragement reached us across every wave. He showed us that fatherhood isn't defined by proximity, but by the strength of a love that withstands time, distance, and sacrifice. He remains our anchor and constant protector, and I am endlessly grateful for everything he surrendered for us.
